AI-powered soil health monitoring

Dr. Sarah Okonkwo May 12, 2026 5 min read

Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing soil health monitoring across Africa. New platforms are using machine learning algorithms to analyze soil samples and provide real-time recommendations to farmers.

This technology helps farmers make informed decisions about fertilizer application, crop selection, and irrigation scheduling. Early adopters have reported yield increases of up to 30%.

The platform combines satellite imagery, IoT sensors, and AI to create a comprehensive soil health dashboard accessible via mobile phone.
